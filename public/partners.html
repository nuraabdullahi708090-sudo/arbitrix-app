Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the referral reward?

You earn 20% of the referred user's first qualifying deposit of $100 or more.

What counts as a qualifying deposit?

A confirmed first deposit of at least $100. Smaller deposits do not qualify.

Do I need to trade to earn?

No. Referral rewards are based on qualifying referred-user deposits. No trading is required.

Is there a minimum payout?

No. There is no minimum payout for referral earnings.

How are referral earnings paid?

In USDT on the TRC20 network, after a manual review and payment by our team.

When does a referral qualify?

When the referred user's first deposit of $100 or more is confirmed. Registration and link clicks alone do not qualify.

Are self-referrals or duplicate accounts allowed?

No. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, fraudulent activity and misleading promotion are not eligible.

Can I give investment or trading advice?

No. Partners are not authorized to provide investment or trading advice on behalf of Arbitrix.

Referral Partner Disclosure

Referral partners provide marketing and introductions only. Partners are not authorized to provide investment or trading advice on behalf of Arbitrix. Referral rewards are based on qualifying referred-user activity. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, fraudulent activity and misleading promotion are not eligible.
