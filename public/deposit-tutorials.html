About third-party services

Paybis, Transak and Trust Wallet are independent third-party services, not part of Arbitrix. Arbitrix does not sell cryptocurrency or process card payments directly. Availability, payment methods, fees and identity checks are set by each provider and vary by country.

Before you send

  • Send USDT only on the TRON (TRC20) network.
  • Always copy the deposit address from your own Arbitrix account.
  • Never share your password, OTP, seed phrase or private keys.
